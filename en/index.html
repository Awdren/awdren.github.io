I am a professor at the Faculty of Computing of the Federal University of Mato Grosso do Sul (UFMS), coordinator of its graduate program in Computer Science (PPGCC) for 2025–2026, and coordinator of the Software Engineering Laboratory (LEDES).

I hold a PhD, a master’s degree and a bachelor’s degree from the Federal University of Amazonas (UFAM).

My experience spans the Nokia and Microsoft developer ecosystems, developer communities, and projects with companies and public institutions. My research brings together people, processes and AI in Software Engineering.

I work as an engineer on projects developed with the Court of Justice of Mato Grosso do Sul (TJMS) and the State Attorney General’s Office (PGE-MS).

Project areas include finding case law, automating the collection of initial claim information, extracting information from legal proceedings, and supporting the preparation of legal responses.

I also coordinate the Technological Residency in Intelligent Software Engineering, connecting professional education to these institutional challenges.

My role: software engineering within the projects and residency coordination. These areas describe partnership scope; the studies below are related research.

Related research and supervision

A project started in 2025 investigates case analysis and legal document production, particularly for healthcare claims, alongside expert calculations and data integration. Related student research examines agentic AI architecture risks, security tactics for AI architectures and requirements discovery using language models.

Higher education · Graduate studies · Research funding

Information to support
public decisions.

Since 2024, I have participated in projects on AI for public governance in higher education and an observatory of Brazil’s National Graduate Education System.

Their objectives include supporting the national advanced training agenda and monitoring education and intellectual output across all 27 Brazilian federative units. The AI initiative is part of CAPES’s strategic institutional project P12.

Since 2023, I have coordinated research on project management in research funding foundations, using BPMN process modeling to support requirements and business rules for a management platform.

Related supervision

Related topics include public-data transparency, in Wallynson Rodrigo Henrique da Silva’s 2022 undergraduate thesis, and AIOps architecture for software product monitoring, in Rafael Rogado Ribeiro Sobrinho’s master’s research started in 2025.
